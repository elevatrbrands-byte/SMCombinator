Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

The Dallas Entrepreneurial Alliance ("DEA") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information you provide through our website, events, and programs.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details (name, email, phone) submitted through forms
  • School affiliation and program interests
  • Event registrations and attendance
  • Anonymous website analytics (device, browser, pages visited)

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide requested services
  • Process event registrations and program applications
  • Send program updates, newsletters, and resources (with opt-out)
  • Improve our programs and website experience

Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with trusted partners who assist in delivering DEA programs, subject to confidentiality agreements, or as required by law.

Cookies & Analytics

DEA uses cookies and analytics tools to understand website traffic and improve content. You can adjust your browser settings to disable cookies; some features may be limited.

Data Security

We implement reasonable safeguards to protect personal information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Rights

You may request access, updates, or deletion of your personal information by contacting privacy@dallasea.org. You may unsubscribe from communications at any time.

Children's Privacy

DEA programs serve high school students. We collect limited information with consent from students, parents/guardians, or schools as appropriate.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy periodically. The revised date will appear at the top of this page.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Contact our privacy team at privacy@dallasea.org.
