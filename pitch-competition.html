What is the Pitch Competition?

The DEA Pitch Competition is the premier entrepreneurship event for Dallas high school students. This is your opportunity to present your business idea to a panel of accomplished judges including venture capitalists, successful entrepreneurs, and business executives who have built companies from the ground up.

Whether you're developing a tech startup, a social enterprise, or an innovative product, our competition provides the platform, feedback, and resources you need to take your idea to the next level. Competitors receive expert coaching in the weeks leading up to the event, pitch in front of a live audience, and compete for significant prize packages.

This isn't just a competition—it's a launchpad for your entrepreneurial journey.

How to Prepare Your Pitch

Successful pitches include these key elements.

✓Problem Statement - Clearly define the problem your business solves
✓Your Solution - Explain your product or service and why it's unique
✓Target Market - Identify your customers and market size
✓Business Model - How will you make money?
✓Traction/Validation - Show evidence of customer interest or early sales
✓Financial Projections - Realistic revenue and cost projections for 3 years
✓Team Introduction - Who's building this and why are you qualified?
✓Funding Ask - If seeking investment, specify the amount and use of funds
✓Visual Pitch Deck - 10-15 slides, professional design, clear charts
