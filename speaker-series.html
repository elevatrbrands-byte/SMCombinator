DEA Speaker Series

Throughout 2025

Learn from Dallas's Most Successful Business Leaders

View Upcoming Events

Access the Minds That Built Dallas

The DEA Speaker Series brings Dallas's most accomplished business leaders directly to you. This isn't about watching from the sidelines—it's about intimate conversations, real advice, and the opportunity to ask questions that matter to your entrepreneurial journey.

Throughout 2025, we're hosting exclusive sessions with titans of industry: NFL franchise owners, generational business dynasties, breakthrough innovators in clean energy, and founders who've built companies from garage startups to household names.

Each session is designed to be interactive and personal. You'll hear origin stories, learn about failures that led to success, understand how to build teams, raise capital, and navigate the challenges that every entrepreneur faces. These aren't generic motivational speeches—these are masterclasses from people who've done it.

All DEA chapter members receive priority access to speaker events. Sessions include Q&A periods, networking opportunities, and in many cases, small-group breakout discussions.

How to Attend Speaker Events

  1. Join a DEA Chapter – Chapter members receive priority registration and exclusive access to certain events.
  2. Register Early – Events fill quickly. Sign up for our email list to receive early notification.
  3. Participate Actively – Prepare thoughtful questions and engage during Q&A sessions.
  4. Network – Stick around after sessions to connect with speakers, judges, and fellow students.

Event Formats

  • ✓Keynote Sessions (100-200 attendees): Full presentations with moderated Q&A.
  • ✓Fireside Chats (50-80 attendees): Intimate conversations with audience participation.
  • ✓Roundtable Discussions (15-25 attendees): Small group deep dives on specific topics.
  • ✓Virtual & Hybrid Sessions: Join from anywhere via live stream.
