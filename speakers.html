About the Series

These aren't typical career talks. DEA speakers share the real stories behind building companies, making high-stakes decisions, and navigating failure. Students hear directly from people who have taken risks, built teams, and created lasting impact across industries.

Each session includes student Q&A, small group discussions, and networking opportunities. It's a rare chance to learn from decades of experience compressed into a single afternoon.

Featured Speakers

Jerry Jones

Owner, President & General Manager, Dallas Cowboys

Built one of the world's most valuable sports franchises by combining bold vision with relentless execution, transforming the Cowboys brand into a global enterprise.

Caroline Hunt

Executive, Hunt Consolidated

Shares the Hunt family's legacy of entrepreneurship across energy, real estate, and philanthropy, and how the next generation is investing in Dallas's future.

Kevin Fine

Chief Commercial Officer, Lunar Energy

Discusses building renewable energy ventures, scaling hardware companies, and driving innovation in sustainable power.

Maya Delgado

Founder & CEO, Beacon Health Tech

Health-tech entrepreneur focused on equitable access to care, sharing lessons on building mission-driven companies.

About DEA

The Dallas Entrepreneurial Alliance was founded to address a simple gap: talented, ambitious high school students with ideas but no clear pathway to develop them. By connecting students with Dallas's business community, we provide more than education—we provide access.
