About the Series

These aren't typical career talks. DEA speakers share the real stories behind building companies, making high-stakes decisions, and navigating failure. Students hear directly from people who have taken risks, built teams, and created lasting impact across industries.

Each session includes student Q&A, small group discussions, and networking opportunities. It's a rare chance to learn from decades of experience compressed into a single afternoon.

Featured Speakers

About DEA

The Dallas Entrepreneurial Alliance was founded to address a simple gap: talented, ambitious high school students with ideas but no clear pathway to develop them. By connecting students with Dallas's business community, we provide more than education—we provide access.
