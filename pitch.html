What to Expect

The DEA Pitch Competition is a full-day experience where selected student entrepreneurs present their concepts to a panel of accomplished judges. Participants refine their pitches through workshops, network with fellow innovators, and compete for resources that can accelerate their ventures.

This isn't just about winning—it's about articulating your vision, defending your assumptions, and building something that lasts.

Eligibility

  • Currently enrolled high school student (grades 9-12) in the Dallas-Fort Worth area
  • Original business concept or venture
  • Individual or team applications accepted (maximum of four members per team)

Competition Format

Round 1: Written Application

Submit your business concept, market analysis, and financial projections through our online portal. Applications are reviewed by the DEA selection committee.

Round 2: Semi-Finals

Selected teams present five-minute pitches followed by Q&A. Top finalists advance to the championship round.

Round 3: Finals

Finalists deliver refined ten-minute presentations before the full judging panel and a live audience. Winners are announced at the closing ceremony.

About DEA

The Dallas Entrepreneurial Alliance was founded to address a simple gap: talented, ambitious high school students with ideas but no clear pathway to develop them. By connecting students with Dallas's business community, we provide more than education—we provide access.
