Our Mission

The Dallas Entrepreneurial Alliance exists to democratize access to entrepreneurial education and opportunities for high school students across North Texas. We believe that the next generation of business leaders shouldn't be limited by their zip code or school district.

By bringing world-class speakers, structured pitch competitions, and comprehensive educational resources directly to students, we're creating pathways to success that didn't exist before. Our goal is simple: ensure every motivated student in Dallas has the tools, knowledge, and connections to pursue their entrepreneurial dreams.

We're building more than a program—we're building a community of young innovators who will shape Dallas's economic future.

How DEA Works

Students gain real-world entrepreneurial experience through an accessible three-step journey.

01
🏫

Join or Start a Chapter

Students can either join an existing DEA chapter at their school or work with us to establish a new chapter. We provide all the resources, curriculum, and support needed.

02
💡

Learn & Develop

Participate in our speaker series, attend workshops, and develop your business idea with guidance from mentors and educational materials.

03
🎤

Compete & Launch

Put your skills to the test in our annual pitch competition. Present to real investors and judges, receive expert feedback, and compete for funding to launch your venture.
