Launch Your Chapter in 5 Steps

Step 1

Submit Application

Complete our online application with basic information about your school, proposed chapter leadership, and faculty advisor. Review timeline: 1 week.

Step 2

Approval & Kickoff Call

Receive your welcome packet and schedule a kickoff call with your dedicated coordinator to discuss goals and resources.

Step 3

Recruit & Organize

Use DEA marketing materials to recruit students, elect officers, set meeting schedules, and plan your semester.

Step 4

Launch Programming

Start meetings using DEA curriculum, host speakers, and begin developing student business ideas with mentorship.

Step 5

Compete & Grow

Prepare students for the pitch competition, participate in speaker series events, and expand your chapter's impact.
