Why Start a Chapter?

Starting a DEA chapter transforms your school into a hub for entrepreneurial education. Chapter leaders receive everything needed to create a thriving program—structured curriculum, access to the DEA network, eligibility for the pitch competition, and direct connection to our speaker series.

Chapters can operate as clubs, class supplements, or full courses. We provide flexibility to fit your school's needs and your students' interests.

What You Get

Complete Chapter Package

  • Founding chapter materials and branding guidelines
  • Curriculum resources covering business fundamentals (optional)
  • Access to the mentorship network
  • Invitations to all DEA events and speaker sessions
  • Direct pathway to the pitch competition
  • Connection to other chapter leaders across DFW

Chapter Models

The Club Model

Weekly or bi-weekly meetings where students work on their own ventures, learn business concepts, and prepare for competitions. Minimal time commitment for a faculty advisor.

The Classroom Integration

Supplementary materials enhance existing business, economics, or STEM courses. Modules can be taught standalone or woven into current curriculum.

The Full Course

Comprehensive semester or year-long entrepreneurship course with lesson plans, case studies, project frameworks, and assessment tools.

Curriculum Resources

Optional modules cover opportunity identification, business model development, financial projections, pitch storytelling, go-to-market strategy, and team leadership.

How to Start

  1. Complete the chapter interest form
  2. Receive your chapter package and onboarding
  3. Launch your chapter and start building

Requirements

  • Faculty advisor willing to sponsor the chapter
  • Minimum of ten interested students
  • School administration approval
  • Commitment to one academic year

Support

Dedicated Guidance

Chapter leaders receive a dedicated DEA staff contact, monthly advisor calls, and access to an online community of fellow educators.

Network Access

Gain access to guest speakers, mentors, and partner organizations ready to invest in the next generation of Dallas entrepreneurs.

About DEA

The Dallas Entrepreneurial Alliance was founded to address a simple gap: talented, ambitious high school students with ideas but no clear pathway to develop them. By connecting students with Dallas's business community, we provide more than education—we provide access.
