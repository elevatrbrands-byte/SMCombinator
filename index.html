What is the Dallas Entrepreneurial Alliance?

The Dallas Entrepreneurial Alliance (DEA) is a comprehensive program designed to inspire, educate, and empower high school students across North Texas to pursue entrepreneurial ventures. We believe that Dallas's next generation of business leaders is in our classrooms today.

Through our two flagship programs—an intensive pitch competition and an exclusive speaker series—we provide students with real-world experience, mentorship from accomplished business leaders, and the tools to transform their ideas into reality.

DEA partners with schools throughout the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex to bring entrepreneurial education directly to students, creating chapters that serve as launchpads for young innovators.
