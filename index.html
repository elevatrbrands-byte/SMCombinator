What is DEA?

The Dallas Entrepreneurial Alliance is a youth-driven initiative designed to cultivate entrepreneurial thinking among high school students across the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex. Through our annual pitch competition and speaker series featuring industry titans, we provide students with direct access to the business community that has made Dallas one of America's most dynamic economic centers.

We believe entrepreneurship isn't just about starting companies—it's about creative problem-solving, resilience, and the courage to build something new. DEA gives students the platform to develop these skills alongside peers who share their ambition.

About DEA

The Dallas Entrepreneurial Alliance was founded to address a simple gap: talented, ambitious high school students with ideas but no clear pathway to develop them. By connecting students with Dallas's business community, we provide more than education—we provide access.
